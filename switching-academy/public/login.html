📢 Switching Academy now uses accounts

More and more students, families and classrooms are using Switching Academy, so we've added sign-in accounts. Accounts help keep the site running smoothly and keep it safe for young learners.

Switching Academy is still free. The lessons, labs and badges haven't changed.

More about this change

New here? Tap Request access. We'll review your request and email you an invitation with a link to set your password.

Your progress now follows you. Once you sign in, your progress is saved to your account, so it shows up on any computer, tablet or phone. If this browser already has progress saved from before, we'll ask whether to add it to your account. Progress codes and backup files still work too.
